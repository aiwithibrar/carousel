Yes! CarouselForge is completely free to use with no hidden costs, no signup walls, and no watermark on exports. All themes, fonts, and download formats are available to everyone.

You can export multi-page LinkedIn PDF documents (ideal for native LinkedIn slider posts) or download all slides as high-resolution PNG images packaged in a single ZIP file.

Yes! You can jump straight into the Studio and start creating immediately without registering an account or entering personal details.
