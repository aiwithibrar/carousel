Why Create Instagram Grids & Panorama Swipes?

A photo grid splits a single impactful photo across multiple posts on your profile feed. When visitors land on your Instagram account, they see a giant, breathtaking mural that instantly establishes a premium brand presence.

  • Instant Visual Wow Factor: A 3x3 grid makes your profile look curated by a high-end creative agency.
  • Boosts Dwell Time: Visitors spend significantly more time scrolling and admiring complete panoramic artwork.
  • Seamless Carousel Swiping: Slicing panoramas into 2, 3, or 4 continuous swipe cards encourages users to swipe all the way through, which is a major positive ranking signal for the Instagram algorithm.

In What Order Should You Post Instagram Grid Photos?

Because Instagram pushes older posts down and places new posts at the top-left, you must post your sliced photos in reverse order:

  1. Start with the Bottom-Right tile (Tile #9 in a 3x3 grid).
  2. Post Tile #8 (bottom-middle), then Tile #7 (bottom-left).
  3. Continue with the middle row (Tile #6, #5, #4).
  4. Finish with the top row (Tile #3, #2, and finally Tile #1 at the top-left).

Our tool automatically numbers every tile and names the files inside your ZIP so you can publish without any guesswork!
