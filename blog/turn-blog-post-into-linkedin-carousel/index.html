How to Turn Your Blog Post Into a LinkedIn Carousel (Step by Step)

By CarouselForge Team · June 14, 2026 · Free Guide

You spent hours writing that blog post. It got some traffic, some shares — and then it disappeared into the internet void.

Here's the thing: your blog post is a goldmine for LinkedIn content. A single 1,500-word article can become 3–5 LinkedIn carousels, each of which can reach thousands of new people.

This guide shows you exactly how to do it in 5 minutes.

Why Repurpose Blog Posts as Carousels?

LinkedIn's algorithm heavily rewards carousel posts (uploaded as documents). They get:

  • 3x more reach than text-only posts
  • Higher save rates — people bookmark carousels to refer back to
  • More profile visits — your handle appears on every slide
  • Longer time-on-post — each swipe counts as engagement

And the best part: you already did the hard work writing the blog post. The carousel is just a reformatted version of content you already have.

Step 1 — Pick the Right Blog Post

Not all blog posts work equally well as carousels. The best ones to repurpose are:

Great for carousels:

  • ✅ List posts ("7 tips for...", "5 mistakes to avoid...")
  • ✅ How-to guides with clear steps
  • ✅ "What I learned" or lessons posts
  • ✅ Case studies with a clear before/after
  • ✅ Industry insights or surprising stats

Less ideal for carousels:

  • ❌ Long narrative stories (hard to condense)
  • ❌ Very technical deep-dives (too much info per slide)
  • ❌ Product announcements (low educational value)

For this guide, let's say you have a blog post titled: "7 Email Marketing Mistakes Killing Your Open Rates"

Step 2 — Extract the Key Points

Open your blog post and identify the core takeaways. For a carousel, you need:

  • 1 strong title/hook (your cover slide)
  • 5–8 key points (one per slide)
  • 1 closing slide with your name and CTA

From our example blog post, the carousel outline would be:

Cover: 7 Email Marketing Mistakes Killing Your Open Rates

1. Using a boring subject line
   Most emails are deleted based on the subject line alone.
   
2. Emailing too frequently
   Sending daily emails trains subscribers to ignore you.

3. No mobile optimization
   60% of emails are opened on mobile. Unreadable = deleted.

4. Generic "Dear Subscriber" greetings
   Personalization increases open rates by 26%.

5. No clear CTA
   Every email needs exactly one job. Give it one button.

6. Ignoring your unsubscribe data
   High unsubscribes signal a content-expectation mismatch.

7. Not A/B testing subject lines
   A 5% improvement in open rate compounds massively over time.

Closing: Follow me for more email marketing tips — @yourhandle

Key rule: Each slide should have a title (the mistake/tip) and 2–3 lines of explanation. Nothing more.

Step 3 — Format It for CarouselForge

Now take your outline and format it as a numbered list. This is what you'll paste into the tool:

7 Email Marketing Mistakes Killing Your Open Rates

1. Using a boring subject line
Most emails are deleted based on the subject line alone. Spend 20% of your writing time on the subject line.

2. Emailing too frequently
Sending daily emails trains subscribers to ignore you. 2-3 emails per week is the sweet spot for most audiences.

3. No mobile optimization
60% of emails are opened on mobile. If it's hard to read on a phone, it gets deleted.

4. Generic greetings
Personalization increases open rates by 26%. Use first names at minimum.

5. No clear CTA
Every email needs exactly one job. One button. One link. One action.

6. Ignoring unsubscribe data
High unsubscribes signal a content-expectation mismatch. Survey leavers — their feedback is gold.

7. Not A/B testing subject lines
A 5% improvement in open rate compounds massively over time. Test every single email.

Step 4 — Generate Your Carousel

  1. Go to the free LinkedIn Carousel Generator on CarouselForge (or use our Instagram Carousel Maker for IG sized posts)
  2. Paste your formatted content into the text box
  3. Click Generate Slides
  4. The tool auto-detects the numbered list format and creates one slide per point

You'll see a live preview instantly. If you need a more basic walkthrough, check out our guide on how to create a LinkedIn carousel in 60 seconds.

Step 5 — Choose Your Design

Pick a theme that matches your personal brand or industry:

  • Midnight / Charcoal — best for B2B, marketing, finance
  • Ocean / Forest — good for wellness, environment, coaching
  • Coral / Candy — great for creative, lifestyle, food brands
  • Minimal — clean and professional for any industry

Pro tip: Add your LinkedIn handle in the Author field. It will appear on your slides, so when people screenshot and share your carousel, they'll always know it came from you.

Step 6 — Download and Post

  1. Click Download All (ZIP)
  2. Unzip the folder — you'll have PNG files, one per slide
  3. Combine them into a PDF using ilovepdf.com
  4. Go to LinkedIn → Create post → Click the document icon
  5. Upload the PDF
  6. Write your caption (use the hook structure from earlier)
  7. Post it

The whole process: About 5 minutes once you have your blog post.

Maximising a Single Blog Post

One blog post can actually become multiple carousels. Here's how to squeeze more out of it:

From one blog post Create
Main tips/points Carousel 1: The tips list
Your personal story Carousel 2: Lessons I learned
Common mistakes you mention Carousel 3: Mistakes to avoid
Tools/resources you mention Carousel 4: Best tools for X
Key stats in the post Carousel 5: Surprising stats about X

That's 5 LinkedIn posts from one piece of content. Posted over 5 weeks, that's a month of LinkedIn content from a single blog post you already wrote.

One piece of content. Four channels. Multiple weeks of posts.
